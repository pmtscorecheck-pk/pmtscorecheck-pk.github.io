PMT Score Check: How Your Score Decides Housing Eligibility in Pakistan

Last updated: 7 November 2026

A PMT Score Check is the quickest way to find out whether your household qualifies for government support in Pakistan. You enter your 13-digit CNIC on the 8171 web portal, or send it by SMS to 8171, and the system tells you what your record shows. The same score is also used by housing schemes such as Apna Ghar Apni Chhat, so checking it first can save you a wasted application.

What a PMT Score Check actually tells you

The check does not hand you a number on its own. It tells you whether the household record linked to your CNIC is marked eligible, and whether that record is complete. If the record is missing or out of date, the result can look wrong even when nothing about your circumstances has changed. That is why the check is useful before you apply for anything: it shows you the record the decision will be based on, while there is still time to fix it.

How the score is worked out

PMT stands for Proxy Means Test. There is no single payslip or bank statement behind it. Instead, the score is built from household survey data collected through the NSER survey: family size, sources of income, the type of house you live in, education and work, and the assets and utilities a household has. Taken together, these details give officials a picture of which families are most likely to need help, even where income is hard to prove on paper. In general, a lower score points to greater need.

Because the score comes from the survey record, it only changes when that record changes. A new job, a move, or a change in family size will not show up until the household is re-surveyed and the record is updated.

How to do a PMT Score Check by CNIC

There are two official routes, and both use the same record, so the result should be the same either way.

  1. On the 8171 web portal: open the portal, enter your 13-digit CNIC, complete the captcha step, and submit. Your eligibility result appears on screen.
  2. By SMS: send your 13-digit CNIC, with no dashes and no extra words, in a text message to 8171. Wait for the reply and keep it, as it is the record of what the system said on that day.

Before you send anything, double-check every digit of your CNIC. A single wrong digit is one of the commonest reasons for a "record not found" message. If you get stuck, the official helpline on 0800-26477 can help during working hours.

Where Apna Ghar Apni Chhat fits in

Housing schemes receive far more applications than there are homes, so they need a fair way to prioritise. The PMT score gives them a common starting point, alongside your CNIC details, your survey status, and the scheme's own rules. The score on its own does not approve or reject a home; it is one part of the record. The housing guide to Apna Ghar Apni Chhat PMT Score verification explains how the two are connected and what to check before you apply.

Check your score before the housing deadline, not after it. If the record needs correcting, the fix takes time, and a scheme will not wait for a re-survey to finish.

If your result looks wrong

Do not try to fix the record through a random website or an agent, and never pay anyone who promises a faster result. The only way to change the official record is through official channels. Visit your nearest BISP office, ask for a household re-survey if your details have changed, and take your CNIC and supporting documents with you. Once the survey is updated, the score can be recalculated.

Mistakes that waste the most time

Most failed checks come down to a handful of avoidable errors: typing the CNIC wrong, sending the SMS to a number other than 8171, or trusting a third party who claims they can change the score for a fee. Use only the 8171 portal, SMS to 8171, or a BISP office. Anything else is not part of the official system, and money paid to an agent will not change your record.

A note on this guide

This page is a plain-language guide, not an official government publication. Eligibility decisions are made on the official record held by BISP and the survey authorities. For the result that counts, always use the 8171 portal, SMS to 8171, or your nearest BISP office.
